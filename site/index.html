FOUR STEPS TO YOUR FIRST HELLO

Your name.
Your people.
Your little circle.

The connected development app is where you manage your profile, friends and privacy. The browser works now; the desktop source adds optional edge pop-ins.

  1. 01

    Make yourself at home

    Sign in, choose a username and display name. Initials work today; your free personal character is the next signup milestone.

  2. 02

    Choose each other

    Opt into exact-username discovery. Find your friend, send a request, and wait for their acceptance.

  3. 03

    A little hi, or a heart

    Choose Available. Pick one of12 free reactions, preview exactly who gets it, then approve.

  4. 04

    Keep your quiet

    Use Invisible or Do Not Disturb. Remove or block a friend. Desktop pop-ins are off until you enable them.
