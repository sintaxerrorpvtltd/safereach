AFTER CODEX STARTS POKI

Your name.
Your person.
Your little inbox.

The local app is where you manage your profile and connection. This public website never collects them. Sessions are temporary and reset when the server restarts.

  1. 01

    Open your inbox

    Codex starts Poki and gives you a local address. Enter a first name and agree to receive pokes.

  2. 02

    Pair a second tab

    Create an invitation. Open it in a separate tab, create a second profile, then accept from the first tab.

  3. 03

    Send a hello

    Use the send control, or prepare with the experimental connector and approve the exact preview in your inbox.

  4. 04

    Stay in control

    View your chosen person and recent hellos. Mute or disconnect. One friend per profile in this prototype.

A remote friend cannot join a localhost invitation. There is no hosted profile dashboard yet. A reviewed remote connection is the next milestone, not a hidden setup step.
