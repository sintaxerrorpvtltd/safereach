# Poki — draft technical policy requirements

**Draft only. Not approved legal terms, legal advice or an active service contract.**
Poki's name is provisional. No business address, company registration identifier,
support contact or legal approval is invented here. No account-creation, billing or
real integration flow is available on the static Pages release.

## Current static release

The Apache-2.0 license applies to the public core code/assets. The static demo
contains Alex and Sam as fixed fictional people, keeps state only in the tab and
does not send messages, collect contacts, store browser data or run visitor
analytics. GitHub handles normal static hosting requests under its own policies.
Open-source licensing is separate from future hosted-service terms and private
operational data. Some1 is a separate commercial product, not open sourced here.

## Required before real sign-in or hosted Pro

- **Operator and contact:** verify the company's correct legal identity, jurisdiction,
  service operator and real privacy/support/abuse contact details before publication.
- **Plain-language consent:** show the exact channel, scopes and recipient; record
  reciprocal acceptance and per-send approval. No inferred consent from browsing,
  model suggestions, internet activity or the name of a contact.
- **Service/privacy terms:** obtain appropriate review, establish permitted users,
  regions and age requirements, and separate necessary service data from optional
  analytics. No analytics should be silently added to a widget or SDK.
- **Private data:** define collected fields, encryption/key boundaries, processors,
  retention/deletion and export behavior. Do not ingest unrelated chats, orders,
  health/location data or broad account histories as part of a hello.
- **Account and channel security:** prove account ownership, keep provider secrets
  server-side, issue scoped/revocable credentials and protect account recovery.
  Channel unlinking must stop future routing and invalidate pending authorizations.
- **Recipient controls:** blocking, DND and disconnect must be enforced before every
  action. Rate limits and abuse handling must not rely only on model instructions.
- **Delivery language:** distinguish queued, received by app and seen by human. No
  implicit read receipts, guaranteed attention, emergency response or uptime claim.
- **Provider compliance:** verify current Instagram DM and Telegram eligibility,
  permissions, messaging restrictions and data policies before enabling adapters.
  WhatsApp is outside this MVP. No unofficial bypass or account scraping.
- **Commercial Pro:** decide actual paid features, pricing, usage limits, renewal,
  cancellation/refund and support terms only when a service exists. Name each
  proprietary module separately; do not restrict the existing Apache core by stealth.
- **Host separation:** participating sites must opt in, users must link identities
  explicitly, and private host business logic/customer records must remain separate.
  Some1 would be a future first-party Pro customer, not shared production resources.

## Publication gate

Do not label this draft “Terms accepted” or require a visitor to agree to it for a
fictional demo. No real sign-in/OAuth/connect/payment flow should ship until the
relevant policy, implementation and provider requirements have been reviewed and
verified. New terms, contacts and data collection require an explicit reviewed
change; this document authorizes none of those operations.
